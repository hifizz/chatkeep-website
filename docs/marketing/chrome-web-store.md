## Title
ChatKeep – AI Chat Manager & Export Tool

## Short Description
The missing OS for your AI chats. Aggregate, organize, search, and export conversations.

## Long Description
ChatKeep is the missing OS for your AI chats.

Stop losing important answers. ChatKeep saves your chats locally, lets you highlight text and add notes, adds a TOC minimap for long threads, and provides fast search.

FEATURES
- Local-first auto-save for Gemini, ChatGPT, DeepSeek, and Grok
- Highlight text and add notes
- TOC minimap navigation (coming soon)
- Search across saved chats
- Export to Markdown (PDF/HTML/JSON coming soon)

PRIVACY
Your data stays on your device. No selling, no training, no tracking.
