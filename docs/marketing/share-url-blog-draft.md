## Why Share Exists

In real collaboration workflows, teams usually hit three problems when sharing AI chats:

1. Copy-paste loses context.
2. Access control is weak after sharing.
3. Link lifecycle is hard to manage.

Share is built to make controlled sharing a first-class product workflow, not a workaround.

## Share as a Feature Family

We split Share into three sub-features:

1. Share URL
2. Share Image
3. Share Malware

This article covers the first two, with Share URL as the main topic and Share Image as a preview.
We will publish a dedicated deep-dive post for Share Image later.

## 1) Share URL (Main focus)

Share URL solves one specific job: sharing a chat quickly and safely without losing structure.

### What users can do

1. Create a shareable link in one flow.
2. Choose access mode: Public or Access Code.
3. Set expiration policy (3/7/30 days or never).
4. Copy and send share info to collaborators.
5. Revoke or delete existing shares from management.

### Why it matters

1. Preserves conversation structure better than screenshots and fragments.
2. Keeps access controlled with access code and expiration.
3. Supports lifecycle control with centralized management and revocation.

### Important note

Share URL is a ChatKeep capability. It is independent of third-party chat platforms and their providers, and does not imply their endorsement.
